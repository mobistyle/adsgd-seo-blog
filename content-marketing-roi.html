Attribution Models for Content

Single-touch attribution (first or last click) undersells content marketing. Implement multi-touch attribution to understand how content contributes across the buyer journey:

  • Linear attribution — equal credit to all touchpoints
  • Time decay — more credit to recent interactions
  • Position-based — 40% to first and last touch, 20% distributed among middle
  • Data-driven — algorithmic distribution based on actual conversion paths

Content Performance Dashboard

Build a centralized dashboard that combines data from Google Analytics, Search Console, and your CRM. Platforms like ADS.GD offer integrated analytics that connect content performance with SEO metrics, making it easier to identify which content assets drive the most value.

Calculating Content ROI

The basic formula: (Revenue from Content - Cost of Content) / Cost of Content × 100

Include all costs: writer fees, design, distribution, tools, and management time. Track revenue through UTM parameters, dedicated landing pages, and CRM attribution.

Optimizing Underperforming Content

Not every piece will be a winner. Audit your content library quarterly:

  • Update outdated statistics and examples
  • Improve internal linking to boost page authority
  • Add multimedia (video, infographics) to increase engagement
  • Consolidate thin pages into comprehensive resources
